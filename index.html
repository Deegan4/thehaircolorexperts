Our Story

A Boutique Salon Built on Craft

Since 2014, The Hair Color Experts has been a full-service boutique salon and signature workshop in Cape Coral. We are a close-knit group of master stylists and colorists who treat every head of hair as a canvas.

We believe great color is equal parts science and artistry. That's why we invest constantly in our craft — staying ahead of the latest techniques, products, and trends so you always leave with a result that turns heads.

  • Personalized consultations with every appointment
  • Master colorists who specialize in corrective work
  • A relaxed, welcoming boutique atmosphere
  • Home to a respected industry education salon
